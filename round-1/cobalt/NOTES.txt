authorized -> reserved
captured -> settled (subtracted from the balance)
voided -> released

Definitions:
- `balance` is the amount of money in the accout, irrespective of any active holds.
- `available` is the amount of 'spendable' money not currently held.
- `authorized hold` doesn't affect the `balance`, but affects the `available` spendable amount.
- `captured hold` will go further to affect the `balance` thereby 'sealing' the transaction.
- `voided hold` will released the 'hold' on the available balance, freeing up the 'put aside' amount.

Life cycle:
hold: held -> captured or held -> voided
- this means a `captured` hold cannot transition to `voided`, and vice versa.

- calling capture more than once on an already captured hold should do nothing (confirm with hold status), same thing for void.
- attempting to capture a hold with a voided hold status should throw an exception, and vice versa.
